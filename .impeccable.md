# Design Context

## Product
A Solana web app for "paired prediction duels." A market is a head-to-head question such as
"Will Apple be more valuable than Nvidia on December 31?" The YES side trades against tokenized
Apple stock (AAPLx) and the NO side trades against tokenized Nvidia stock (NVDAx). Trading fees
are collected in those stock tokens and paid to the holders of each side. Tagline energy:
"Bet on Apple. Get paid in Apple."

## Target audience
- Solana traders who already use pump.fun, StonkFun, Jupiter and Phantom. They trade from a
  phone or a second monitor, skim fast, and judge a product in the first five seconds.
- Hackathon judges (Colosseum, Solana Foundation) watching a three-minute demo video who
  reward functionality, novelty, UX and composability.
- Crypto-native people who follow stocks (xStocks users) and want a reason to hold.

## Use cases
1. Open a duel, see the odds and the resolution date, place a bet in one signature.
2. Hold a side and watch stock-token payouts land in the wallet a few times a day.
3. Sell or add to a position; understand the odds impact before signing.
4. Create a new duel from a template (A vs B by market cap, A outperforms B, A above a price).
5. On resolution day, redeem the winning side for a dollar each.

## Brand personality / tone
Fight night meets the financial broadsheet. Confident, terse, a little theatrical. A boxing
poster's "tale of the tape" and a racetrack tote board printed on cream paper with black and
vermilion ink. Not a neon terminal, not a SaaS dashboard, not a casino. Serious about numbers,
playful about the matchup. Sports-page copy: "Apple leads 54–46." "Paid 3.1 AAPLx to 412 holders."

## Aesthetic direction (committed)
Letterpress tote board. Warm paper background, near-black ink, one vermilion accent that is
also the color of the right-hand side of every duel. Condensed black display type for matchups,
tabular numerals everywhere, hairline and double rules instead of cards, rubber-stamp status
labels, split-flap odds that flip when they change. Asymmetric broadsheet grid with a strong
left rail. Light theme only.

## Hard constraints from the founder
- No generic AI fonts (no Inter, Roboto, Arial, system stacks).
- No gradients, no glassmorphism, no glow, no drop-shadow cards, no card grids.
- Reuse existing headless component primitives for behavior (dialogs, selects, sliders,
  tabs); all visual styling is ours.
- Must look genuinely designed. If it could pass as default AI output, it is wrong.

## Stack
Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4 with CSS variables in oklch, Radix
primitives for behavior, Solana wallet-adapter, Meteora cp-amm SDK, Pyth. Demo mode with
fixture data so every screen is browsable without a wallet.
